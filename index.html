About

I'm an M.S. Computer Science student at Full Sail University, focused on data science with machine learning and AI engineering close behind. Before grad school, I worked in web design — which is part of why I care as much about whether a model's output is usable as whether it's accurate.

My coursework and capstone work span the full stack of a data science project: pulling and cleaning real data, building and comparing models, evaluating them honestly, and shipping the result behind an interface someone can actually use. Recent work includes a cold-start recommendation system, a mixed-frequency forecasting model for currency markets, and several applied ML systems in computer vision and NLP.

Projects

Capstone · in progress

Salamander

A reading-recommendation app that tackles the "cold-start" problem — giving new users relevant book suggestions before the system knows their taste — while keeping the results diverse across genres and authors. Built on a shared sentence-embedding space with a dual-centroid design: one feedback-weighted centroid drives recommendations, a second unweighted centroid audits how much genre and author diversity is on offer.

  • React
  • FastAPI
  • Supabase / pgvector
  • Sentence-Transformers

Capstone · plan B

Raven

A forecasting study on whether geopolitical risk (GPR) improves short-term CAD/USD prediction. Compares a Mixed-Frequency Transformer against ANN and LSTM baselines under three conditions — no GPR signal, GPR resampled to daily frequency, and GPR left at its native mixed frequency — to see whether keeping the risk signal's real cadence actually helps.

  • Python
  • TensorFlow / Keras
  • GARCH (arch)
  • FRED & Yahoo Finance data
